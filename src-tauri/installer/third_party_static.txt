Crates inside nsis_tauri_utils.dll
----------------------------------

nsis_tauri_utils.dll is the crate nsis-tauri-utils 0.5.3 built for i686-pc-windows-msvc as a
no_std library. Besides its own code and the workspace crate nsis-plugin-api (MIT OR Apache-2.0,
the LICENSE_MIT above), it contains core and alloc of the Rust standard library (licence in the
entry "Rust standard library (std, core, alloc)") and the three crates below. The proc-macro
crate nsis-fn and its dependencies proc-macro2, quote, syn and unicode-ident run at build time
only. The versions are the newest releases on crates.io that matched the requirements of the
workspace's Cargo.toml files on 26 Jan 2026, the day the DLL was built: semver "1.0" with
default features off, windows-sys "0.61.2", and windows-link "^0.2.1" through windows-sys.
